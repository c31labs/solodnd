# Security policy

Lanternbound stores private player accounts, character information, and adventure journals. Responsible disclosure is welcomed and player privacy comes first.

## Report a vulnerability

Do not publish suspected vulnerabilities, personal information, access tokens, credentials, exploit details, or screenshots containing private data in a public GitHub issue.

Submit a private security report through the contact route at `https://direwrought.com/`. Include:

1. The affected page or component.
2. A concise description of the issue and realistic impact.
3. Reproduction steps using only an account and data you control.
4. Relevant browser, device, and timing information.
5. A safe method for contacting you if you want a response.

## Authorised research boundaries

Good faith review is limited to ordinary use of the Official Service and passive review of publicly displayed source. Unless C31 Labs gives prior written authorisation, do not:

1. Access, alter, retain, or disclose another person’s data.
2. Use automated scanners, denial of service techniques, excessive traffic, credential attacks, social engineering, or destructive payloads.
3. Test GoDaddy, Brevo, GitHub, or another third party service through Lanternbound.
4. Bypass authentication or continue testing after confirming a vulnerability.
5. Demand payment, threaten disclosure, or make a report public before remediation has been coordinated.

## Our response

Reports will be assessed according to severity, exploitability, and player impact. C31 Labs will make reasonable efforts to acknowledge a valid report, investigate it, protect affected players, and communicate an appropriate remediation timeline.

No bounty, payment, employment, licence, or waiver is promised. Good faith activity that remains strictly within this policy will not be treated as malicious by C31 Labs, but this statement cannot authorise conduct involving systems or rights owned by another party.

## Supported version

Only the current Official Service at `https://direwrought.com/lanternbound/` is supported. Copies, forks, mirrors, and unauthorised deployments are unsupported and are not authorised by the project licence.
